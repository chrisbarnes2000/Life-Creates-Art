# **App Name**: MiniBarnMaster

## Core Features:

- Shed Designer: A step-by-step interactive tool allowing users to customize their shed, including dimensions, roof style, doors, and material selections like CDX plywood and T1-11 siding.
- Instant Quote: Provide an immediate estimated price based on the user's customized shed design selections.
- PNW Material Guide: Information pages detailing the benefits and durability of specific materials (CDX plywood, T1-11 siding) for the Pacific Northwest climate.
- Weather-Resilient Design Assistant: An AI tool that suggests optimal shed configurations or specific material considerations based on user-provided details about their property's location and local climate within the Pacific Northwest.
- Warranty Information Display: Clearly present the 5-year guarantee on materials and craftsmanship provided by MiniBarnMaster.
- Consultation Request Form: Allow users to submit their customized shed design for a detailed consultation or a formal quote from MiniBarnMaster.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A robust, professional blue (#264073), symbolizing durability and trust, suitable for headlines and important interactive elements.
- Background color: A subtle, light grey-blue (#F0F2F4), providing a clean and open canvas for designs, while maintaining harmony with the primary color.
- Accent color: A vibrant, clear aqua (#47D0EB), chosen to highlight calls to action and critical information, drawing the eye without being distracting.
- Headlines and body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, clean, and objective aesthetic, ensuring legibility and a contemporary feel across all content.
