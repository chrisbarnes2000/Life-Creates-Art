# **App Name**: LifeCreatesArt

## Core Features:

- Fine Art Photo Gallery: Interactive fine art photo and album viewer showcasing collections, series, and standalone photography by Tina Barnes.
- Lightbox & Watermark: Immersive lightbox viewer with custom watermarking and high-resolution view support.
- Consultation & Inquiries: Secure consultation request form allowing users to connect and commission or purchase artwork.
- Admin Master Control: Enterprise management for gallery items, site branding, hero carousel slides, and customer operations.
- Testimonials & Reviews: Public review submission and moderation system.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated, elegant tone suitable for fine art portfolios and creative showcases.
- Background color: A clean, neutral canvas for showcasing high-end imagery.
- Accent color: A warm, refined highlight color for CTAs and critical information.
- Typography: Distinctive headline and clean body font pairing for high readability and premium aesthetics.
